Fitness · prototypes for vertical slices

One standalone prototype per slice, sharing tokens and components from shared/. Everything shown is sample data for design review. Open any slice on a phone or a 390px-wide window; on desktop a panel on the left switches states (offline, empty, errors, workout in progress).

Suggested build order

  1. Sign in + onboarding: auth, the profile and the weekly-frequency goal everything else reads.
  2. Plans: routines and the exercise library feed both Today and the workout.
  3. Today + the workout log: the core loop, offline first.
  4. History: reads finished sessions, edit and delete recalculate PRs and streak.
  5. Progress and Me: charts, body, goals and settings on top of the data above.

Copy is English with en-US numbers to match the workout log prototype; PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md still describe a Vietnamese UI. Outside a workout the app uses a bottom tab bar (Today, Plans, History, Progress, Me); inside a workout there is none.
